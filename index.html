Selected work

2025 – 2026MSc dissertation · LSE

Sustainability on the Move

Pro-environmental practice and horizon security under chronic liminality · supervised by Dr Atrina Oraee

Behavioural read

Sequential mixed-methods study of international postgraduates who had moved home more than three times in three years: 65 eligible survey responses screened from 181, then 12 interviews analysed with reflexive thematic analysis. Built on the habit discontinuity hypothesis, social practice theory and Installation Theory.

Impact read

Explains why recycling, energy and food habits collapse in short-let and shared housing, even among people who care. Certainty about how long a home will last matters more than attitude does. That puts the problem with landlords, universities and housing providers, not with tenant education campaigns.

The contribution

A dependency–horizon model: a 2×2 separating how far a practice depends on its physical setting from how certain a person is about that setting lasting. Horizon security splits into duration certainty (how long do I have) and succession certainty (do I know what comes next), and the two behave differently.

Feb 2023 – presentUBC Visual Cognition Lab

The Correlation Project

Advisor since Jan 2026; previously Project Leader, Secondary Project Leader and Co-Pilot · PI Dr Ronald Rensink

Behavioural read

Psychophysics of how precisely and accurately people perceive correlation in scatterplots, using just-noticeable-difference discrimination and direct estimation. My own COGS 402 study tested whether those judgements hold up when mark shape and opacity change.

Impact read

Impact and ESG reporting assumes the reader takes the right relationship out of a chart. This work measures how far that holds when the design changes. It also gave me four years of running a research programme and then handing it over.

How it ran

Experiments run on a locally hosted Node.js application, extending the lab's existing JavaScript codebase. Analysis in Excel using Spearman and Pearson correlation with sensitivity checks. I recruited, onboarded and trained each intake of Co-Pilots, and now advise the project rather than lead it.
